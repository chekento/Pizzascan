🍕 PizzaScan

Welcome to PizzaScan!

This app helps you find pizza spots around the world using OpenStreetMap data.

To show your current location on the map and find pizza nearby, please allow GPS access.

Map tiles, visible map bounds and searches are sent to OpenStreetMap services. GPS is optional; saved places and ratings stay on this device. Navigation opens an external app.
